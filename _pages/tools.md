For now feel free to check out my Open-Source Tools on [Codeberg.org](https://codeberg.org/n04n6).

Why Codeberg.org and not github.com?

I once started with GitHub but was disappointed once they locked my account without any reason and deleted all my content. I for sure got an offline copy / remote repo for all my public tools but this was very unfortunate...

This is why I choosed and supported the Codeberg Project, hosted in Germany with the goal to support independed developers and hoste the service in the EU with the open-source platform Forgejo (Fork of GitEA).

I will provide a detailed overview of all my tools in the future but for now, feel free to access all my tools on [Codeberg.org](https://codeberg.org/n04n6).
